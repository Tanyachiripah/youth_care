# YouthCare Project Overview

## What YouthCare is

YouthCare is a web-based youth support and empowerment platform for Zimbabwe. Its guiding statement is **“No Young Person Should Struggle Alone.”** The project aims to make it easier for young people to discover wellbeing information, practical guidance, verified support providers, and education or employment opportunities.

## Problem and response

Support services and opportunities can be difficult to discover or navigate. YouthCare is intended to bridge that information gap by bringing directories, educational content, referrals, and signposting together in one accessible place. The platform is an entry point to information and human services, not a substitute for them.

## Mission, vision, and audience

- **Mission:** help young people find useful information and appropriate next steps without judgement.
- **Vision:** a future in which young people can discover and access trusted support and opportunities when they need them.
- **Intended users:** young people in Zimbabwe, support organisations, and platform administrators. The initial age range and safeguards for minors still require research and professional consultation.

## Intended social impact

The intended outcomes are improved awareness of support options, easier discovery of verified providers, more informed and consent-based referrals, and better visibility of learning and employment opportunities. These are goals, not measured outcomes; the demonstration has no impact-evaluation data.

## Current scope and limits

The repository is a demonstration MVP. It contains account registration and login, a basic personal dashboard, a provider directory, resources, opportunities, referral requests, an AI information/signposting endpoint, and partial administration APIs. It does not provide clinical care, a monitored crisis service, confirmed partner integrations, or a full case-management system. Seed listings are explicitly demonstrations and are not real providers.
